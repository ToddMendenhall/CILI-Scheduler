Backup & restore

Export

Downloads every project, tool, person and piece of equipment as a single file, for keeping your own copy or handing it to someone. The schedule itself is saved on the server, which also keeps a snapshot from the start of each day.

Import

Restores from a backup file. You choose whether to merge it into what is already here, or replace everything. Either way the change is saved for everyone in your organization.

Backups are plain JSON — readable and safe to archive
